# forge.acme

`forge.acme` is Forge’s fake-company extension. It is the living proof that a new organization can create meaningful workflows without forking or modifying the Forge core.

## Owns

- Marketing, Finance, Design, and Engineering workflows.
- Acme manifests, versioned prompts, policies, fixtures, sandbox profiles, themes, and acceptance demos.
- The flagship `engineering-feature` fixture and the A1–A5 scenario suite.

## Does not own

- The workflow compiler, LangGraph integration, queues, provider implementations, sandbox lifecycle, approval persistence, or generic control-plane UI.
- Any Gusto- or Buzz-specific configuration.

## What it proves

Acme runs the same Forge runtime through public SDKs only. Its scenarios demonstrate campaign approval, finance denial, read-only design work, sandboxed engineering change, and cross-domain execution. The first practical engineering proof begins through CLI/API intake and becomes a reviewed, policy-controlled, PR-ready artifact.
